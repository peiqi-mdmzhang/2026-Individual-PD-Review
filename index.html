1. Motivation 🧡
＋
How am I inspiring my pupils to want to learn?
  • Cultivating Personal Value and Joy: I strive to inspire a love for Mathematics by framing mathematical concepts within a continuous narrative world called Aridara. To prevent stories from being read and discarded, students became active "citizens" tasked with saving a kingdom which was slowly losing its magic. They solved problems across multiple units — navigating puzzles in the ruined kingdom for "Decimals", attending the Super Aridara Primary School to learn "Rate" with the main characters and calculating GST/discounts at the Super Aridara Power Supermarket.
  • Promoting Student Voice and Fun: On Google Classroom, I regularly post engaging riddles, mathematical fun facts and encouraging messages alongside instructional notes. This ongoing online presence builds a positive classroom culture and offers students low-pressure ways to express themselves and take charge of their learning journey.

2. Participation in a Community 💬
＋
How am I ensuring every learner is included in the learning process?
  • Constructivist Learning: From time to time, I invited my students to discover mathematical concepts through hands-on discovery. For example, for the topic "Volume", students were given the opportunity to build cube structures (using physical cubes and an online interactive) to help with their visualisation of the top/front/side views. They also tried to investigate collaboratively to discover the formula for the volume of a cuboid.
  • Inclusive Platforms and Co-dependent Roles: Hands-on group tasks required students to rely on one another to complete investigations. Additionally, Google Classroom served as an inclusive platform for all students — especially the more soft-spoken ones — to ask questions, access gamified practice and share their thinking asynchronously.

3. Challenge ⚙️
＋
How am I stretching every learner to their next level of growth?
  • Tiered and Accessible Support Structures: To ensure every student can complete learning tasks independently, I recorded custom instructional videos and uploaded them to Google Classroom. For students who struggled to access the online resources, I attached physical QR codes directly onto their worksheets, scaffolding immediate access and fostering long-term self-management.
  • Low-Pressure Parent Partnerships: To support student growth beyond the classroom without overwhelming families, I shared learning resources via the ClassDojo portfolio. Parking resources in a central, accessible hub allows parents to support their child's learning needs at their own convenience without the pressure of compulsory follow-ups.
  • Extending Learning Opportunities: To address time constraints in class and provide further challenge, I created an online interactive version of the Aridara Supermarket activity. Students selected items of their choice, calculated the prices after GST/discount and earned a sticker version of their selected item through a weekly classroom delivery service.
  • Reflection: While student participation on digital platforms naturally fluctuates throughout the year, posting periodic activities and targeted prompts helped sustain a core group of self-directed learners who consistently seek extended learning opportunities.
